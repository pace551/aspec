# ADR Template

Copy to `decisions/adr-NNNN-{slug}.md` (framework-level) or `{project}/docs/adr/` (project-
level, per `DEV-DOCS`). One decision per file. Keep it under a page — an ADR is a record,
not an essay.

````markdown
# ADR-NNNN: {Decision title, imperative or noun phrase}

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-MMMM
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: James (+ Claude session link if useful)

## Context

{2-6 sentences: the forces in play. What problem, what constraints, what makes this
non-obvious enough to record.}

## Decision

{1-3 sentences, declarative: "We will …". The one part people quote.}

## Alternatives considered

- **{Alternative}** — {why rejected, one line}

## Consequences

{What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we're now committed to, any follow-ups.}
````
